## Reference credits and process

- Centaurian Arsenal, EIT Clothiers and CJ’s screenshot references: [Enjar — Exploring New Atlantis](https://enjargames.com/starfield-or-exploring-new-atlantis), screenshots credited by that article to Enjar.
- Rowland Arms exterior screenshot reference: [Shawn Robinson / Prima Games — Where to Buy Ammo in Starfield](https://primagames.com/gaming/where-to-buy-ammo-in-starfield).
- Starfield environments, branding and game art originate with Bethesda Game Studios.

The built-in image generation tool removed HUD elements and people, improved lighting and reconstructed empty item presentation areas. These are AI-adapted/reimagined scenes, not unmodified screenshots. The Rowland interiors are imagined from its exterior architecture and branding. Original reference screenshots are not bundled in the mod. Credits identify the sources; they do not assert a new license to their original screenshots.

Each prompt requested a landscape 16:9 empty environment, clear central space for a separately rendered item, no HUD or hero item, restrained detail and shop-specific architecture. Helmet scenes use a display stand; suits/apparel use fitting alcoves; packs use an equipment pedestal. Selected PNGs were encoded as WebP at quality 92. No selected scene replaces the theme defaults. Ground contact and lighting must still be reviewed with real items in game.
