# Scanner Visibility
# Extends hand-scanner base range to 100 and scan range to 50 for easier spotting and scanning.
# Keeps highlight colors unchanged. Existing skills and mods may further modify range.
# Use Reset Scanner Visibility for the documented base-game values.

# Scanner base range
setgs fHandScannerBaseRange 100

# Scanning range
setgs fHandScannerScanRange 50
