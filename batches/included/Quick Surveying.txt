# Quick Surveying
# Sets required plant and animal survey counts to 1 for subsequent scans.
# Does not automatically survey planets. Use the scanner on each species.
# Use Reset Quick Surveying to restore the base-game counts (8 each). Other mods may choose different counts.

# Flora samples required
setgs iHandScannerPlantsCountBase 1

# Fauna samples required
setgs iHandScannerAnimalCountBase 1
